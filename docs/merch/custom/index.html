HOW TO ORDER

Make it yours.

Two ways to create your selfdriven merch.

Start with a design.

A little selfdriven, ready to personalise.

  1. Choose a selfdriven design template.
  2. Select your garment colour, size and quantity in Screenlab.
  3. Check the details and click “ADD TO CART”.
Browse designs ↗

Start with a blank.

Bring your own idea to life.

  1. Open Screenlab and “Choose A Blank”.
  2. Select your garment and click “CUSTOMISE”.
  3. Click “Images” and upload your chosen selfdriven artwork.
  4. Finish your design and click “ADD TO CART”.

From Australia, to you.

Screenlab is based in Australia and offers global delivery. Check current pricing, production times and shipping at checkout.
